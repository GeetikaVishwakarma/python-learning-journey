# Practice Questions – Loops

## Beginner

1. Print numbers from 1 to 10 using a `for` loop.
2. Print numbers from 10 to 1.
3. Print all even numbers from 1 to 20.
4. Print all odd numbers from 1 to 20.

---

## For Loop

1. Print each character of your name.
2. Create a list of five fruits and print each fruit.
3. Print the multiplication table of a number.
4. Find the sum of numbers from 1 to 100.

---

## While Loop

1. Print numbers from 1 to 10 using a `while` loop.
2. Print even numbers from 1 to 20.
3. Calculate the sum of numbers from 1 to 50.

---

## Break and Continue

1. Print numbers from 1 to 10 but stop when the number reaches 6.
2. Print numbers from 1 to 10 but skip number 5.

---

## Nested Loop

```text

1 3
2 1
2 2
2 3
3 1

```

## ⭐ Challenge

Write a program to print the multiplication tables from 1 to 10.

Example:

```text
2 x 1 = 2
2 x 2 = 4
2 x 3 = 6
...
2 x 10 = 20
```
